faq_title: "Frequently Asked Questions"
faqs:
  - q: "What is purchase order software?"
    a: "Purchase order software lets a business create, approve, send, and track purchase orders digitally. It replaces spreadsheets and paper forms, records who approved each purchase, and links every PO to the goods received and the vendor bill that follows."
  - q: "Does a small business really need purchase orders?"
    a: "Once more than one person can commit company money, or you buy from more than a handful of suppliers, a PO process pays for itself. It prevents unauthorised and duplicate purchases, gives you a written agreement on price and quantity, and tells finance what bills are coming before they arrive."
  - q: "What is 3-way matching?"
    a: "3-way matching compares the purchase order, the goods receipt, and the vendor bill before payment. A bill is only approved for payment when the quantities and prices on all three agree, within any tolerance you set."
  - q: "Is a purchase order recorded in the general ledger?"
    a: "No. An approved PO is a commitment, not a liability, so it does not create a journal entry. The liability is recorded when goods or services are received or the vendor bill is posted. Good PO software still tracks open commitments so you can see them against your budget and cash forecast."
  - q: "Can I handle partial deliveries and back orders?"
    a: "Yes. Each goods receipt is recorded against the PO line, so a PO can be received in several deliveries. The remaining quantity stays open until it is received or you close the line."
