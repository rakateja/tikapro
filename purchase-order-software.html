Why Purchasing Breaks as You Grow

When the owner approved every purchase personally, you didn't need a system. Once managers, outlets, or project teams start buying, WhatsApp messages and spreadsheets stop being enough. These are the leaks we see most often in growing businesses:

Maverick spending

Staff order directly from suppliers with no approval. Finance only finds out when the bill arrives — after the money is already committed.

Duplicate & inflated bills

Suppliers bill twice, charge a higher price than quoted, or invoice for items that never arrived. Without a PO to compare against, nobody notices.

No view of what's committed

Your bank balance looks healthy, but you have weeks of orders in transit. Cash-flow surprises come from commitments you couldn't see.

Weak audit trail

Who approved this purchase? At what price? When auditors or tax officers ask, the answer is buried in chat history and email.

From Request to Payment in One Flow

TikaPro follows the procure-to-pay cycle that finance teams in larger companies rely on — simplified so a small team can run it without a dedicated procurement department.

  1. Request

    Staff raise a purchase request with items, quantities, estimated cost, department, and the reason for the purchase.

  2. Approve

    The request is routed to the right approver based on amount, department, or category. Approvals work from any device.

  3. Issue PO

    Approved requests become numbered POs with agreed prices, tax, delivery date, and payment terms — sent to the supplier as a PDF.

  4. Receive

    Your warehouse or outlet records what actually arrived, line by line. Partial deliveries keep the rest of the PO open.

  5. Match & Bill

    Convert the PO into a vendor bill in one click. TikaPro compares PO, receipt, and bill and flags any difference.

  6. Pay

    Only matched, approved bills are scheduled for payment according to the supplier's terms. Everything posts to your ledger.

Is It Time for a PO System?

If two or more of these sound familiar, a purchase order process will pay for itself quickly.

  • More than one person can place orders with suppliers
  • You buy regularly from more than 10 suppliers
  • You run multiple outlets, sites, or projects
  • Bills arrive that nobody remembers ordering
  • Supplier prices change without anyone noticing
  • Month-end close is delayed chasing missing invoices
  • Your auditor has asked for purchase approval evidence
  • You want to delegate buying without losing control

Frequently Asked Questions

What is purchase order software?

Purchase order software lets a business create, approve, send, and track purchase orders digitally. It replaces spreadsheets and paper forms, records who approved each purchase, and links every PO to the goods received and the vendor bill that follows.

Does a small business really need purchase orders?

Once more than one person can commit company money, or you buy from more than a handful of suppliers, a PO process pays for itself. It prevents unauthorised and duplicate purchases, gives you a written agreement on price and quantity, and tells finance what bills are coming before they arrive.

What is 3-way matching?

3-way matching compares the purchase order, the goods receipt, and the vendor bill before payment. A bill is only approved for payment when the quantities and prices on all three agree, within any tolerance you set.

Is a purchase order recorded in the general ledger?

No. An approved PO is a commitment, not a liability, so it does not create a journal entry. The liability is recorded when goods or services are received or the vendor bill is posted. Good PO software still tracks open commitments so you can see them against your budget and cash forecast.

Can I handle partial deliveries and back orders?

Yes. Each goods receipt is recorded against the PO line, so a PO can be received in several deliveries. The remaining quantity stays open until it is received or you close the line.
